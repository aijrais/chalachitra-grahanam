Studio guide

Review a clip

Open a local file, play or scrub to a moment and choose Add event. Select a timeline row to replay it or edit its time, evidence and review status. Space toggles playback and M adds a marker outside form fields.

Basketball

Mark the left and right rim edges. Automatic analysis looks for moving orange balls passing down through a fixed hoop. Occlusion, a moving camera or another ball colour can hide makes. Confirm the basket and assign its point value, team or player when visible.

Carnatic violin

Use a solo held note. Pa uses 3:2 above Sa and Upper Sa uses 2:1. Custom accepts a teacher’s target frequency. Gamakas, finger positions and accompanied passages need other evidence.

Save and restore

Analysis supports up to 60 seconds. Longer files can be reviewed manually. Export JSON, then import it and reattach the same recording on another device. Session drafts stay in this browser. Your media stays on your device.
